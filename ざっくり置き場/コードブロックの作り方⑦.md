# Flower Logo Prompts + Negative Prompts
## House Ririka
```text
Prompt:
Create a dark elegant symbolic emblem for House Ririka, based on the Nigella flower. Calm, intelligent, distant, structured, observatory-like crest, silver-blue and cold white palette on a dark background, centered composition, delicate radiating lines, subtle circular geometric framing, refined and untouchable, premium symbolic logo.
Negative prompt:
cute style, bright cheerful colors, pastel palette, chibi, cartoon, casual doodle, overly soft fantasy, excessive romance, flashy glitter, thick heavy ornament, messy layout, low detail, cluttered background, playful mood, warm orange dominance, gaudy gold, childish emblem, noisy composition, comic style
```

## Red Ririka
```text
Prompt:
Create a dark elegant symbolic emblem for Red Ririka, based on the Gloriosa flower. Invasive, seductive, intense, controlled, dangerous and refined, deep crimson, wine red, black red, subtle dark gold on a dark background, centered crest composition, sharp curling flame-like silhouette, premium ceremonial symbolic logo.
Negative prompt:
cute style, bright cheerful colors, pastel palette, chibi, cartoon, soft innocent floral mood, excessive romance, sugary beauty, playful emblem, rounded childish forms, overly cute heart motifs, cheap fantasy logo, neon rainbow colors, messy ornament, light casual design, comic style, low detail, clipart look
```

⸻

さらに使いやすい「共通negative prompt」

3つ全部に共通で雑に使いたいなら、これでもOK。

cute style, bright cheerful colors, pastel palette, chibi, cartoon, comic style, childish design, clipart look, messy composition, low detail, cluttered background, excessive sparkle, overly romantic mood, playful tone, cheap fantasy look

⸻

使い分けの感覚

* Prompt
    →「こうしてほしい」
* Negative prompt
    →「これは入れないで」

って役割だね。
